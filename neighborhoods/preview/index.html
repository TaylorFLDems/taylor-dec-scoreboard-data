How we count

Each number is the share of registered Democrats in that neighborhood who have voted so far. We only show percentages, and every neighborhood is big enough that no one household stands out.

Neighborhood lines are drawn by Taylor Democrats for this scoreboard. They are not precincts or official districts, and some cross precinct lines. Votes cast on Election Day are added after the county updates its records.

Source: daily voter files from the Taylor County Supervisor of Elections.
